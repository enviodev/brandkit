## 01. Logo

The Envio mark across its four primary contexts.

![Envio Logo Variants: Primary, On Mid Gray, On Gradient, On Light](https://github.com/Jordy-Baby/brandkit-preview/raw/main/logos/envio-logo-showcase.png)

### Variants

| Variant | When to use | File |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Primary | Default. Light and neutral backgrounds. | `envio-logo-primary.svg` / `.png` |
| On Mid Gray | Mid-tone backgrounds and cards. | `envio-logo-primary.svg` / `.png` |
| On Gradient | Brand gradient backgrounds. Use the white wordmark. | `envio-logo-white.svg` / `.png` |
| On Light | Light backgrounds where the gradient mark reads cleanly. | `envio-logo-primary.svg` / `.png` |
| Icon (1:1) | Social avatars, favicons, app icons. | `envio-logo-1x1-clear.svg` / `.png`, `envio-logo-1x1-black.png` |
| High-res | Print and large-format use. | `envio-logo-high rez.png` |

All logo files live in the [logos](./logos) folder. Prefer SVG wherever possible.

### Do's

* Use the white logo on dark, dark-grey, or gradient backgrounds
* Use the dark logo on light or gradient backgrounds
* Maintain clear space equal to the height of the "e" on all sides
* Scale proportionally at all times
* Use the wordmark on all public-facing materials

### Don'ts

* Place the white logo on white or very light backgrounds
* Recolour the logo outside of approved variants
* Stretch, rotate, or skew the mark
* Add drop shadows or filters
* Use the logo smaller than 80px wide in digital contexts

## 05. Voice and Tone

### Four Principles

**1. Direct and Technical.** Envio speaks to builders. No fluff. Specifics matter. "142x faster than The Graph" is better than "blazing fast performance."

**2. Community-First.** Envio is by builders, for builders. The tone is collaborative, never corporate. We ship together.

**3. Transparent.** Benchmarks are sourced. Code is public. Pricing is clear. Trust is built through openness, not marketing.

**4. Momentum.** Copy moves fast. Short sentences. Active verbs.

### Writing Examples

| Use | Avoid |
| --- | --- |
| "Index 4 billion events in 6 days. Not 6 months." | "Our industry-leading blockchain indexing solution leverages cutting-edge technology to deliver exceptional performance." |
| "npm install -g envio. You're already halfway there." | "Begin your journey with our simplified onboarding process by installing our command-line interface." |
